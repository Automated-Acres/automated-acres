# Homestead Systems

This area will contain technology projects tied directly to the physical operation of a homestead.

Planned topics include:

- irrigation
- water systems
- pumps and tanks
- livestock equipment
- doors and gates
- feeders
- environmental controls
- equipment monitoring
- property-wide automation

These projects may interact with machinery, animals, water, and other real-world systems. Designs should therefore fail safely and explain manual override, verification, and rollback behavior where applicable.

> **Status:** Structure established; projects will be added as they are built and tested.
