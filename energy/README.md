# Energy

This area will contain Automated Acres projects for measuring, understanding, and managing homestead energy use.

Planned topics include:

- solar production
- battery systems
- generators
- inverter and charger monitoring
- wall-power measurement
- load management
- performance per watt
- energy dashboards
- efficiency experiments
- backup-power behavior

Projects involving electrical equipment must document safety assumptions, measurement methods, and verification steps clearly.

> **Status:** Structure established; projects will be added as measurements and procedures are validated.
